OpenCode Mobile

Support

An unofficial app for using your own OpenCode server from your phone.

Contact

Send bug reports, questions and requests by email. In the app, Settings → Contact us opens a mail with the app version and OS already filled in.

support@opencodemobile.app

The app is made by one person, so a reply can take a few days.

Common questions

The app can't reach my server.
Check that your phone and the OpenCode server are on the same network, and that the server accepts outside connections (for example opencode serve --hostname 0.0.0.0).

Notifications don't arrive.
Check that the opencode-mobile-push plugin is installed on the server and paired in the app, and that notifications are allowed for the app in your phone's settings.

I want "Remove ads" on another device.
On a device signed in with the same Apple ID or Google account, open Settings → Restore purchase.
