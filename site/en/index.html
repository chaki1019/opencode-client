Your OpenCode,
from your phone.

OpenCode Mobile connects your iPhone or Android phone to the OpenCode server running on your own PC or Mac. Away from your desk, you can still send instructions, follow progress and approve what the agent wants to do.

What it does

Chat

Pick a session and send instructions. Replies and tool runs stream in live, along with todo progress and command output.

Permissions and questions

Approve file edits and commands, and answer the agent's questions from your phone, so work doesn't stall until you are back at your desk.

Push notifications

Get notified when a reply finishes, fails, waits for permission or asks a question. Notifications are decrypted on your phone, so the relay can't read project names or titles.

Review changes

Check changed files, diffs and branches. Browse and search files, or open a terminal.

Switch models and agents

Change the model or agent on the spot, attach images, or stop a running reply.

  • Several serversSave your home PC and work Mac and switch between them.
  • Your data stays with your serverChats and files travel only between the app and your server. The developer never receives them.

How to use it

  1. Start the OpenCode server on your PC or Mac

    Update OpenCode, choose a password, and start the server so other devices can connect.

    OPENCODE_SERVER_PASSWORD=your-password \
  opencode serve --hostname 0.0.0.0 --port 4096
  2. Connect from the app

    Servers on the same network show up in the list automatically. If yours doesn't, enter its URL (for example http://192.168.1.10:4096). Enter the username (opencode by default) and the password, then tap Connect. The password is kept in the Keychain / Keystore.

  3. Pick a project and a session

    Projects you have opened in OpenCode are listed. Open a session to carry on, or start a new one.

  4. Turn on notifications (optional)

    Tap the bell at the top right of the project list, turn notifications on, paste the settings it shows into ~/.config/opencode/opencode.json on your PC, and restart OpenCode. The opencode-mobile-push plugin is installed from npm automatically.

To connect from outside your home, we recommend a VPN such as Tailscale rather than exposing the server to the internet. The app works with servers that have the OpenCode v2 API.

Price

Free to use. A banner ad appears on the project and session lists (never in chat). After 10 messages in a day, watching a video ad lets you send 10 more. A one-time "Remove ads" purchase turns off both.
